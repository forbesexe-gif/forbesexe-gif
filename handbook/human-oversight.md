# Human Oversight

A practical allocation of human review and intervention for high-impact automated decisions.

## Operating practices

- Define which actions require preview, approval, or dual control
- Give operators clear context, alternatives, and rollback
- Measure override, escalation, and missed-intervention patterns

## Review standard

Changes should be evidence-based, scoped for review, explicit about limitations, and reversible where practical. Credentials, private infrastructure details, and unnecessary personal data must remain outside public artifacts.
