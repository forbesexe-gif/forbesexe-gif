# Architecture Decision Records

A lightweight record of consequential technical choices, alternatives, evidence, and reversal conditions.

## Operating practices

- State context, decision, rationale, and tradeoffs
- Record rejected alternatives and unresolved risks
- Revisit decisions when assumptions or evidence change

## Review standard

Changes should be evidence-based, scoped for review, explicit about limitations, and reversible where practical. Credentials, private infrastructure details, and unnecessary personal data must remain outside public artifacts.
