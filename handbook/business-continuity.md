# Business Continuity

Planning for sustained critical operations during provider outages, staffing constraints, security events, and infrastructure failure.

## Operating practices

- Identify critical services, dependencies, and manual fallbacks
- Assign decision authority and communication paths
- Exercise degraded operation and full recovery

## Review standard

Changes should be evidence-based, scoped for review, explicit about limitations, and reversible where practical. Credentials, private infrastructure details, and unnecessary personal data must remain outside public artifacts.
