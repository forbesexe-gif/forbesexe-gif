# Third-Party Integrations

A lifecycle for selecting, authorizing, monitoring, and removing external services.

## Operating practices

- Review permissions, data flow, terms, limits, and failure modes
- Keep provider credentials isolated and revocable
- Plan fallback, migration, and secure decommissioning

## Review standard

Changes should be evidence-based, scoped for review, explicit about limitations, and reversible where practical. Credentials, private infrastructure details, and unnecessary personal data must remain outside public artifacts.
