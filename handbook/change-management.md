# Change Management

Controls for proposing, reviewing, deploying, verifying, and reversing material changes.

## Operating practices

- Classify change risk and required approvals
- Record scope, evidence, rollout, and rollback
- Verify the production result rather than assuming deployment success

## Review standard

Changes should be evidence-based, scoped for review, explicit about limitations, and reversible where practical. Credentials, private infrastructure details, and unnecessary personal data must remain outside public artifacts.
