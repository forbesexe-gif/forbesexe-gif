# Compliance Readiness

A control-evidence approach for preparing projects to meet applicable legal, contractual, and policy obligations.

## Operating practices

- Map obligations to owners, controls, and evidence
- Document scope, exceptions, and remediation dates
- Avoid claiming certification or compliance without independent basis

## Review standard

Changes should be evidence-based, scoped for review, explicit about limitations, and reversible where practical. Credentials, private infrastructure details, and unnecessary personal data must remain outside public artifacts.
