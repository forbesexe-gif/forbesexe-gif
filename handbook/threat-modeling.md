# Threat Modeling

A repeatable process for identifying assets, trust boundaries, plausible abuse cases, and proportionate controls.

## Operating practices

- Map assets, actors, entry points, and trust boundaries
- Prioritize threats by likelihood, impact, and exposure
- Track mitigations, residual risk, and validation evidence

## Review standard

Changes should be evidence-based, scoped for review, explicit about limitations, and reversible where practical. Credentials, private infrastructure details, and unnecessary personal data must remain outside public artifacts.
