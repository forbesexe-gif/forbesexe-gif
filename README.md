### PinkCharts

PinkCharts is evolving toward reproducible historical simulations, strategy evaluation, risk and behavior analysis, and compliance awareness for registered representatives and other financial professionals.

Compliance-related features are informational; they are not firm supervision, regulatory certification, or personalized investment advice. The current direction does not include live securities execution.

### SHAsweeper and the S23 experiment

SHAsweeper's Bitcoin-focused cryptography and local/regtest research remains separate from the **S23 Merged Miner**, an associated experimental, phone-based Litecoin/Dogecoin project using Scrypt and AuxPoW.

The S23 experiment stays in its own repository and has not been validated as a production-ready miner.

### KoalaWire

KoalaWire's existing authorized security-assessment alpha is preserved while further development and resource-intensive expansion are paused.

## Engineering Principles

- Evidence before claims, reproducible research, and transparent limitations.
- Human oversight for consequential decisions and external actions.
- Privacy, security, least privilege, and explicit authorization.
- Deterministic validation for calculations, system behavior, and release gates.
- Modular, maintainable software with bounded infrastructure costs.
